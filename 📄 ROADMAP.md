# MGaut Ark System – Roadmap

The MGaut Ark System is governed by a three‑covenant model.  
This roadmap outlines the forward path for the **public covenant** only.  
Enterprise and sovereign covenants follow separate, sealed roadmaps.

---

## 🌳 Public Covenant (`main`)

The public covenant is designed to demonstrate the core capabilities of the Ark System while remaining accessible to all operators.  
Planned enhancements include:

- **Accessibility Enhancements**  
  - Expanded support for **visual learners, auditory learners, and individuals with language‑based learning disorders**.  
  - Harmonized multi‑modal feedback (visual, auditory, haptic).  

- **UI/UX Improvements**  
  - Streamlined navigation and touch‑first controls.  
  - Expanded playlist functionality with global persistence.  
  - Improved red‑letter tagging for Gospel and Revelation texts.  

- **Study Tools**  
  - Enhanced Bible study panels with cross‑references.  
  - Notes panel refinements for clarity and auditability.  
  - Expanded etymology search and definition features.  

- **Deployment Stability**  
  - Continuous integration and deployment pipeline refinements.  
  - Automated testing for accessibility and performance.
