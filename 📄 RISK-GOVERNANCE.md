# MGaut Ark System – Risk Governance

The MGaut Ark System treats risk management as a governed, sealed process.  
This file defines how risks are identified, categorized, and mitigated across covenant branches.

---

## 🌳 Covenant Risk Rules

- **Public Covenant (`main`)**  
  - Risks: Deployment stability, accessibility regressions, public vulnerability exposure.  
  - Mitigation: Automated testing, accessibility validation, responsible disclosure via `SECURITY.md`.  
  - Governance: Transparent and visible to all.  

- **Enterprise Covenant (`enterprise`)**  
  - Risks: Integration failures, contractual SLA breaches, licensed feature regressions.  
  - Mitigation: Private monitoring, enterprise escalation channels, contractual remediation.  
  - Governance: Sealed under commercial license.  

- **Sovereign Covenant (`sovereign`)**  
  - Risks: Operator‑grade encryption, sovereign workflows, private doctrine integrity.  
  - Mitigation: Direct operator oversight, sealed changelogs, private audit discipline.  
  - Governance: Fully private, unshared, unbroken.  

---

## 🔐 Risk Governance Principles

1. **Sealed Artifacts**  
   - Each covenant manages its own risk domain.  
   - No public operator may access enterprise or sovereign risk data.  

2. **Audit Discipline**  
   - All risks must be logged, categorized, and tied to releases.  
   - Public risks are visible in GitHub issues.  
   - Enterprise and sovereign risks are sealed under their respective governance.  

3. **Operator Empowerment**  
   - Risk documentation must provide actionable mitigations.  
   - Escalation paths must be reproducible and governed.  

4. **Resilience**  
   - Risk governance ensures fallback, redundancy, and recovery.  
   - Error correction is treated as a governed, auditable process.  

---

## 🚫 Boundaries

- No external contributors may access enterprise or sovereign risk registers.  
- Public covenant risks are limited to the scope of the `main` branch.  
- Enterprise and sovereign risk governance remain sealed.
