# MGaut Ark System – Encryption Governance

The MGaut Ark System treats encryption as a governed, sealed process.  
This file defines how MGaut encryption and related cryptographic protocols are maintained, audited, and enforced across covenant branches.

---

## 🌳 Covenant Encryption Rules

- **Public Covenant (`main`)**  
  - Scope: Public documentation of MGaut principles, open cryptanalysis invitations.  
  - Disclosure: High‑level specifications only, no sensitive keys.  
  - Governance: Transparent and visible.  

- **Enterprise Covenant (`enterprise`)**  
  - Scope: Licensed implementations, enterprise key management, integration with enterprise systems.  
  - Disclosure: Controlled under NDA and license.  
  - Governance: Sealed under commercial license.  

- **Sovereign Covenant (`sovereign`)**  
  - Scope: Full MGaut encryption doctrine, sovereign key hierarchies, operator‑grade protocols.  
  - Disclosure: None.  
  - Governance: Fully private, unshared, unbroken.  

---

## 🔐 Encryption Governance Principles

1. **Sealed Artifacts** – Each covenant enforces its own encryption scope.  
2. **Audit Discipline** – Public covenant invites open cryptanalysis; enterprise and sovereign remain sealed.  
3. **Operator Empowerment** – Operators must control keys, not vendors.  
4. **Ethical Stewardship** – Encryption is deployed to protect privacy and human rights.
