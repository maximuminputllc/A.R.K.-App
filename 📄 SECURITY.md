# Security Policy

The MGaut Ark System is governed by a three‑covenant model.  
Security reporting is permitted only for the **public covenant** (`main` branch).  
The **enterprise** and **sovereign** covenants are sealed and not open to external review or disclosure.

---

## 🔐 Supported Versions

- **`main` (Public Covenant)**  
  - Actively deployed via GitHub Pages.  
  - Eligible for vulnerability reporting.  

- **`enterprise` (Commercial Covenant)**  
  - Licensed deployments only.  
  - Vulnerability reporting handled through private enterprise support channels.  
  - Not open to public submissions.  

- **`sovereign` (Private Covenant)**  
  - Reserved exclusively for the architect/operator.  
  - Not open to any external reporting.  

---

## 🛡️ Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in the **public covenant** (`main` branch):

1. **Do not open a public issue.**  
   Security issues must be reported privately.

2. Send a detailed report to:  
   **Maximum Input, LLC**  
   [Insert your preferred security contact email here]

3. Include:  
   - Description of the vulnerability  
   - Steps to reproduce  
   - Potential impact  
   - Suggested remediation (if known)

---

## ⚡ Response Process

- Reports will be acknowledged within **7 business days**.  
- Valid vulnerabilities will be triaged, patched, and deployed as quickly as possible.  
- Credit may be given to the reporter if desired, unless anonymity is requested.  

---

## 🚫 Out of Scope

- Vulnerabilities in the **enterprise** covenant must be reported through licensed enterprise channels.  
- The **sovereign** covenant is private and not subject to external review.  
- Denial‑of‑service attacks, spam, or social engineering attempts are not considered valid reports.  

---

## 📜 Legal Notice

By submitting a vulnerability report, you agree to:  
- Not disclose the vulnerability publicly until it has been patched.  
- Not exploit the vulnerability beyond what is necessary to demonstrate it.  
- Act in good faith and within the bounds of responsible disclosure.
