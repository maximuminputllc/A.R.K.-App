# MGaut Ark System – Support Policy

The MGaut Ark System is governed by a three‑covenant model.  
This document defines how support is provided for each covenant.

---

## 🌳 Public Covenant (`main`)

- **Scope of Support**  
  - General usage questions about the deployed public version.  
  - Accessibility feedback (visual, auditory, and language‑based learning support).  
  - Bug reports related to the public covenant only.  

- **How to Request Support**  
  - Open an issue in this repository, clearly describing the problem.  
  - Include reproducible steps, expected behavior, and actual behavior.  
  - Do not include proprietary code, screenshots of enterprise features, or requests for sovereign access.  

- **Response Commitment**  
  - Issues will be reviewed on a best‑effort basis.  
  - Security vulnerabilities must be reported privately (see `SECURITY.md`).  

---

## 🏢 Enterprise Covenant (`enterprise`)

- **Scope of Support**  
  - Enterprise features, integrations, and deployments.  
  - Support is provided only under a valid commercial license agreement.  

- **How to Request Support**  
  - Licensed organizations must use their designated enterprise support channel.  
  - Public issue tracker is not available for enterprise covenant support.  

---

## 🔐 Sovereign Covenant (`sovereign`)

- **Scope of Support**  
  - No external support is provided.  
  - This covenant is private, sealed, and reserved exclusively for the architect/operator.
