# MGaut Ark System – Resilience Governance

The MGaut Ark System treats resilience as a governed, sealed process.  
This file defines how redundancy, fallback, recovery, and continuity are enforced across covenant branches.

---

## 🌳 Covenant Resilience Rules

- **Public Covenant (`main`)**  
  - Resilience Scope: Uptime of GitHub Pages deployment, accessibility continuity, and fallback for public study tools.  
  - Mitigation: Automated redeploys, cached assets, accessibility validation.  
  - Governance: Transparent and visible to all.  

- **Enterprise Covenant (`enterprise`)**  
  - Resilience Scope: Licensed deployments, enterprise dashboards, and integrations.  
  - Mitigation: Multi‑region hosting, contractual SLAs, automated failover.  
  - Governance: Sealed under commercial license.  

- **Sovereign Covenant (`sovereign`)**  
  - Resilience Scope: Operator‑grade encryption, sovereign workflows, private doctrine continuity.  
  - Mitigation: Direct operator oversight, sealed backups, private recovery protocols.  
  - Governance: Fully private, unshared, unbroken.  

---

## 🔐 Resilience Governance Principles

1. **Sealed Artifacts**  
   - Each covenant enforces resilience within its own scope.  
   - No public operator may access enterprise or sovereign recovery protocols.  

2. **Audit Discipline**  
   - Recovery drills and fallback tests must be logged and versioned.  
   - Public covenant resilience is transparent.  
   - Enterprise and sovereign resilience remain sealed.  

3. **Operator Empowerment**  
   - Operators must be able to trigger fallback paths with clarity.  
   - Alerts must be actionable and multi‑modal (visual, auditory, haptic).  

4. **Continuity**  
   - Resilience ensures continuity of study, access, and governance.  
   - Error recovery is treated as a governed, auditable process.  

---

## 🚫 Boundaries

- No external contributors may alter resilience processes.  
- Enterprise resilience is contractual and not public.  
- Sovereign resilience is private and never disclosed.
