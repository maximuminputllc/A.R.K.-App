# MGaut Ark System – Compliance Governance

The MGaut Ark System treats compliance as a governed, sealed process.  
This file defines how compliance with standards, regulations, and ethical frameworks is enforced across covenant branches.

---

## 🌳 Covenant Compliance Rules

- **Public Covenant (`main`)**  
  - Compliance Scope: Accessibility standards (WCAG), open web standards, responsible disclosure.  
  - Enforcement: Automated accessibility testing, semantic versioning, transparent changelogs.  
  - Governance: Fully visible and auditable.  

- **Enterprise Covenant (`enterprise`)**  
  - Compliance Scope: Contractual obligations, enterprise security standards, data protection frameworks (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001).  
  - Enforcement: Private audits, contractual reporting, sealed enterprise documentation.  
  - Governance: Sealed under commercial license.  

- **Sovereign Covenant (`sovereign`)**  
  - Compliance Scope: Operator‑defined ethical frameworks, sovereign encryption doctrine, private governance standards.  
  - Enforcement: Direct operator oversight, sealed changelogs, private audit discipline.  
  - Governance: Fully private, unshared, unbroken.  

---

## 🔐 Compliance Governance Principles

1. **Sealed Artifacts**  
   - Each covenant enforces compliance within its own scope.  
   - No public operator may access enterprise or sovereign compliance records.  

2. **Audit Discipline**  
   - Compliance evidence must be logged, versioned, and tied to releases.  
   - Public covenant compliance is transparent.  
   - Enterprise and sovereign compliance remain sealed.  

3. **Accessibility & Ethics**  
   - Compliance includes accessibility for **visual learners, auditory learners, and individuals with language‑based learning disorders**.  
   - Ethical stewardship is encoded as a first‑class principle.  

4. **Operator Empowerment**  
   - Compliance processes must be reproducible, auditable, and enforceable.  
   - Operators must be able to validate compliance outcomes.  

---

## 🚫 Boundaries

- No external contributors may alter compliance processes.  
- Enterprise compliance is contractual and not public.  
- Sovereign compliance is private and never disclosed.
